How to Play Spot It!

  1. Play as a solo detective with a stopwatch. Choose picture or word clues and flashlight size.
  2. Open Settings to load the editable Food Preset or create a new clue set. Add pictures, then save.
  3. Choose the built-in background or upload your own. Uploads immediately change the game background.
  4. Use Edit Object Positions to drag each clue and resize it with the yellow ↘ handle. Arrange clues for your solo detective board.
  5. Start the 3, 2, 1, GO! countdown. Move the flashlight onto a clue first, then tap it while it is illuminated. Finding an item plays bling.mp3 and shows a large flashcard.
  6. When all clues are found, reveal.mp3 plays and the team's score, time, and found-item review appear. Try again to improve your time.
  7. Use Export JSON to share or back up activities and Import JSON to restore them. Uploaded images are embedded in JSON; preset photo paths require the same photos/ folder.
